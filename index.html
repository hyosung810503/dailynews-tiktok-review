Authorized short-form publishing workflow

Prepare, review, and publish approved short-form video from one controlled workflow.

Dailynews Social Publisher is a desktop-assisted publishing service for authorized publishing users. It helps users prepare vertical videos, review publishing information, connect an authorized social account, and submit approved content through supported platform integrations.

What the service does

Content preparation

Authorized users prepare approved short-form video assets, captions, hashtags, and publication metadata before any platform submission.

Account authorization

A supported social account must be explicitly authorized before the service can access permitted account information or publish content.

Publishing controls

Before publishing, the service presents the target account and available publishing settings so the user can confirm the intended submission.

Publication tracking

The service stores publication status and identifiers needed to prevent duplicate submissions and support operational auditing.

Publishing workflow

  1. Prepare. An approved vertical video and caption are prepared in the publishing workflow.
  2. Authorize. The user connects an account through the platform authorization flow and grants only the permissions required for publishing.
  3. Review. The service retrieves creator information and supported privacy options, then displays the target account and publishing settings.
  4. Confirm. The user confirms the publication request for the selected video.
  5. Publish and track. The approved video is transferred through the supported Content Posting API, and the resulting publication status is recorded.

Platform integration

For TikTok publishing, Dailynews Social Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit for account authorization and the Content Posting API for authorized video publishing. The basic-profile permission is used only to identify the authorized account in the publishing interface, and the publishing permission is used only when an authorized user submits a video for publication.

The service does not publish to an account that has not authorized the application. Available privacy settings are obtained from the platform for the authorized creator before publication.

Permissions used

  • user.info.basic — identify the authorized account.
  • video.publish — submit an approved video to the authorized account.

Content rights and source systems

Source content may be supplied under distribution authorization from publishing partners, including idailynews.co.kr. Dailynews Social Publisher does not claim administrative ownership or control of idailynews.co.kr or of a source publisher's domain, website, editorial system, or infrastructure.

The publishing service is separate from the source publisher's website and administrative systems. Content is distributed only where the applicable publishing or distribution authorization has been granted.

Security and user control

Authorization credentials are handled by the publishing workflow and are not intentionally exposed in public pages or published content. An authorized account owner can revoke application access using the platform's account controls. Stored authorization information may also be removed when the connection is no longer required.
