Short-form publishing workflow by Dailynews

Prepare, review, and publish short-form news video from one controlled workflow.

Dailynews Social Publisher is a desktop-assisted publishing service for authorized newsroom and publishing users. It helps users prepare vertical news videos, review publishing information, connect an authorized social account, and submit approved content through supported platform integrations.

See the publishing workflow Visit Dailynews

What the service does

Content preparation

Users prepare Dailynews short-form video assets, captions, hashtags, and publication metadata before any platform submission.

Account authorization

A supported social account must be explicitly authorized before the service can access permitted account information or publish content.

Publishing controls

Before publishing, the service presents the target account and available publishing settings so the user can confirm the intended submission.

Publication tracking

The service stores publication status and identifiers needed to prevent duplicate submissions and support operational auditing.

Publishing workflow

  1. Prepare. A vertical news video and caption are prepared in the Dailynews publishing system.
  2. Authorize. The user connects an account through the platform authorization flow and grants only the permissions required for publishing.
  3. Review. The service retrieves creator information and supported privacy options, then displays the target account and publishing settings.
  4. Confirm. The user confirms the publication request for the selected video.
  5. Publish and track. The approved video is transferred through the platform Content Posting API, and the resulting publication status is recorded.

Platform integration

For TikTok publishing, Dailynews Social Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit for account authorization and the Content Posting API for authorized video publishing. The basic-profile permission is used only to identify the authorized account in the publishing interface, and the publishing permission is used only when an authorized user submits a video for publication.

The service does not publish to an account that has not authorized the application. Available privacy settings are obtained from the platform for the authorized creator before publication.

Permissions used

  • user.info.basic — identify the authorized account.
  • video.publish — submit an approved video to the authorized account.

Security and user control

Authorization credentials are handled by systems controlled by Dailynews and are not intentionally exposed in public pages or published content. An authorized account owner can revoke application access using the platform's account controls. Dailynews may also remove locally stored authorization information when the connection is no longer required.
