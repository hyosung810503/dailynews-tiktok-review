Dailynews Social Publisher

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Dailynews Social Publisher processes information when a user connects an authorized social account and uses supported publishing features.

2. Information processed

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the service may process:

  • basic account identifiers and basic profile information returned by the platform;
  • OAuth access and refresh credentials required to maintain an authorized connection;
  • publishing settings selected or returned for the authorized account;
  • video captions, hashtags, publication identifiers, URLs, status, timestamps, and error information generated during the publishing workflow.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is processed only as needed to:

  • identify the account that the user authorized;
  • maintain the authorized connection;
  • display supported publishing settings;
  • submit user-approved short-form video content;
  • prevent duplicate submissions and maintain publication history;
  • support security, troubleshooting, and operational auditing.

4. Storage and security

Authorization credentials and publication records used by the publishing workflow are protected with access restrictions appropriate to the service. Access tokens and refresh tokens are not intentionally exposed in public pages, public logs, or published content.

5. Sharing and sale of data

The service does not sell authorization data or connected-account data. Information is transmitted to a connected platform only as necessary to perform the authorized integration or as otherwise required by law.

6. Source-content relationship

Source content may be supplied under distribution authorization from publishing partners, including idailynews.co.kr. The source publisher retains control of its own domain, website, editorial systems, and administrative infrastructure.

idailynews.co.kr is not represented by this policy as the operator or administrator of Dailynews Social Publisher, and this service does not claim administrative access to that domain.

7. Retention

Authorization information is retained only while necessary to operate the authorized connection or satisfy legitimate security, audit, and operational requirements. Publication history may be retained as needed to prevent duplicate submissions and maintain operational records. Authorization credentials may be removed when access is revoked or the integration is discontinued.

8. User choices and revocation

An authorized account owner may revoke application access using the controls made available by the connected platform. Users may also contact the service regarding information handled directly by Dailynews Social Publisher.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or supported integrations change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material revisions are made.
